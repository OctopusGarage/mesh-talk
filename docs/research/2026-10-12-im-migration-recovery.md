# Messenger migration and recovery patterns

Investigated 2026-10-12. Sources are first-party product guidance. Product capabilities and wording may change; this note captures interaction and security patterns, not a proposed Mesh-Talk implementation.

## The three user situations

| Situation | Established interaction | Security and data boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Old device available | Signal and WhatsApp put **Transfer from old device** in new-device setup, then use a QR code or matching code and explicit approval on the old device. WhatsApp shows transfer progress, pauses messaging, allows cancellation, and tells users to keep the old device until the new one is confirmed. Signal's primary-device transfer *moves* history and unregisters the old device. [Signal device transfer](https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/10066808631066-Android-to-Android-Device-Transfer), [WhatsApp chat transfer](https://faq.whatsapp.com/209942271778103/) | Possession of the old device authorizes the transfer. The UI should say whether the result is a **move**, a **copy**, or an **additional device**, because these have different consequences. Signal and WhatsApp disallow merging two divergent histories in these flows. [Signal device transfer](https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/10066808631066-Android-to-Android-Device-Transfer), [WhatsApp transfer troubleshooting](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1039228717453239/) |
| Old device lost, backup exists | Signal offers optional encrypted Secure Backups and asks for a saved recovery key during initial setup. WhatsApp restores from an existing cloud backup after account verification and, for end-to-end encrypted backups, the backup password/key. Element offers a recovery key as a way to verify a new device and regain encrypted history and digital identity. [Signal Secure Backups](https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/9708267671322-Signal-Secure-Backups), [WhatsApp restore](https://faq.whatsapp.com/618575946635920/), [Element recovery key](https://docs.element.io/latest/element-support/device-verification/how-to-ensure-you-have-a-recovery-key/) | A sign-in credential alone does not imply access to encrypted history. Signal says even its service cannot restore an archive without the recovery key. WhatsApp says it cannot reset a forgotten backup password if the user no longer has access to chats. Element says the recovery key is separate from the account password. [Signal Secure Backups](https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/9708267671322-Signal-Secure-Backups), [WhatsApp forgotten backup password](https://faq.whatsapp.com/639067727894647/), [Element recovery key](https://docs.element.io/latest/element-support/device-verification/how-to-ensure-you-have-a-recovery-key/) |
| No old device or usable backup/key | Signal allows registration without transfer but cannot recover the unavailable message history. Element offers cryptographic identity reset when neither a recovery key nor verified device exists; previous encrypted messages then cannot be decrypted and contacts see that the identity changed. Telegram restores Cloud Chats on sign-in, but device-specific Secret Chats do not follow and are lost on logout. [Signal transfer and restore](https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/360007059752-Backup-and-Restore-Messages), [Element recovery key](https://docs.element.io/latest/element-support/device-verification/how-to-ensure-you-have-a-recovery-key/), [Telegram FAQ](https://telegram.org/faq) | Offer an honest **start fresh** path with explicit loss and identity-change wording. Do not imply that account access or a surviving secondary device can recover content if the system does not support it; Signal explicitly says linked devices cannot restore history to a phone. [Signal backup troubleshooting](https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/10075139325850-Troubleshooting-Signal-Secure-Backups), [Element FAQ](https://element.io/help) |

## What the user needs to know before relying on recovery

- **Backup state and age:** WhatsApp shows the last backup time and size, and warns that messages after the latest backup cannot be recovered. Its Android help says only the last successful Google Account backup is restorable. [WhatsApp restore](https://faq.whatsapp.com/618575946635920/), [WhatsApp backup](https://faq.whatsapp.com/481135090640375/)
- **Exact scope:** Signal Secure Backups include text history and, on the free tier, only the last 45 days of media; view-once items and messages due to disappear within 24 hours are excluded. WhatsApp's local transfer covers chats, media, and account information but excludes specific payment and channel content. The product should show its own inclusion and exclusion list before export and before restore. [Signal Secure Backups](https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/9708267671322-Signal-Secure-Backups), [WhatsApp chat transfer](https://faq.whatsapp.com/209942271778103/)
- **A recoverable key:** Signal asks users to save and confirm the key during backup setup. Element documents testing a recovery key by verifying a new session and gives a route to replace a lost key while a verified device still exists. This is an established pattern for proactive recovery checks, not only a key displayed once. [Signal Secure Backups](https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/9708267671322-Signal-Secure-Backups), [Element recovery key](https://docs.element.io/latest/element-support/device-verification/how-to-ensure-you-have-a-recovery-key/)
- **Available restore path and timing:** Signal separates device transfer, local backup file, and remote Secure Backup, with different platform limits; restoration is during initial setup and backups cannot be merged. WhatsApp tells users not to wipe the old phone until the new one is verified. [Signal backup options](https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/10074659364122-Backups-and-Device-Transfers-on-Signal), [Signal Secure Backups](https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/9708267671322-Signal-Secure-Backups), [WhatsApp chat transfer](https://faq.whatsapp.com/209942271778103/)
- **Security identity:** Element treats recovery of the encryption identity separately from ordinary account login; if it must be reset, contacts can see the change and may need to verify again. This makes identity continuity a first-class outcome in the recovery UI. [Element FAQ](https://element.io/help), [Element recovery key](https://docs.element.io/latest/element-support/device-verification/how-to-ensure-you-have-a-recovery-key/)

## Interaction principles to apply

1. Present choices by the user's actual situation: **I have my old device**, **I have a backup and recovery key**, **I cannot recover my previous data**. State up front which option preserves identity and history.
2. For live transfer, pair devices with a short-lived visual challenge, require confirmation on the source, show progress and a clear completion check, and preserve the source until destination validation completes. This follows Signal and WhatsApp's documented flows. [Signal device transfer](https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/10066808631066-Android-to-Android-Device-Transfer), [WhatsApp chat transfer](https://faq.whatsapp.com/209942271778103/)
3. For backups, show last successful backup, covered content, storage location, and whether the recovery key has been saved and tested. Make the no-key consequence explicit at setup, not only after loss. [WhatsApp restore](https://faq.whatsapp.com/618575946635920/), [Signal Secure Backups](https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/9708267671322-Signal-Secure-Backups), [Element recovery key](https://docs.element.io/latest/element-support/device-verification/how-to-ensure-you-have-a-recovery-key/)
4. Treat **account access**, **message history**, and **cryptographic identity** as separate outcomes in copy and implementation. Element's identity reset and Telegram's Cloud Chat/Secret Chat distinction demonstrate why a generic “restore successful” label can be misleading. [Element recovery key](https://docs.element.io/latest/element-support/device-verification/how-to-ensure-you-have-a-recovery-key/), [Telegram FAQ](https://telegram.org/faq)

The principles above are an inference from the cited product flows; they are not claims that Mesh-Talk already provides those capabilities.
