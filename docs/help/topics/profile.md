# Change your profile

Open your profile from the sidebar to change your display name, picture or About text.

## Open your profile

1. Select your own avatar or name in the sidebar account area.
2. In **Profile**, choose **Edit name** to change the display name, then save it.
3. Select the large avatar to upload a picture, choose from an available gallery, or remove a custom picture.
4. If uploading a picture, review and confirm the crop before saving.

## Understand identity versus appearance

Your display name is a nickname. Your login username and account ID remain read-only in the profile. Changing the name or picture does not create a new account or prove a person's identity.

Use [contact verification](verify-contact.md) for trust decisions. For more pictures and themes, see [Customization packs](customization-packs.md). Installing an avatar library and selecting an avatar are separate actions.

## Recognize contacts while they are offline

The sidebar, conversation header and group member list use the same display rule:
your private remark takes priority over the contact's latest known name, with a
short account ID used only when no name is known. Going offline does not remove
the last known name or avatar.

Open a member's profile to inspect the original name separately from your remark,
along with the account and device details. A remark changes your local label; it
does not replace the other person's identity information.

## Edit About and share your account

Edit **About** in your profile and select **Save**. It appears in the direct
conversation header and in your contact profile. Your name, picture and About
synchronize automatically; no separate publication or signed file is required.
About supports up to 2,048 UTF-8 bytes; non-Latin characters can use several bytes.

The account creation date is read-only and recorded automatically when the account
is created. Older accounts without a recorded date show **Unknown**. Restoring a
backup or linking a device does not replace the original date.

Expand **Account ID and QR code** to copy or share your account ID. The QR code
identifies the account; it does not prove your real-world identity. See
[profiles and verification](../../peer-trust.md) for evidence limits.
