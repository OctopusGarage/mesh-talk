# Collect useful messages in Favorites

**Favorites** keeps useful messages together with your own searchable notes.
**Message Yourself** is the separate conversation where you send messages to yourself.

## Save one message or a discussion

1. Open a message's context menu and choose **Add to Favorites**.
2. To collect a discussion, choose **Select messages**, select up to 100 messages
   in that conversation, then choose **Collect**.
3. Optionally add a title and overall description. Expand **Message note** to
   explain individual messages without changing their original content.
4. Choose **Save**. Cancel leaves the collection unchanged.

For example, collect a troubleshooting discussion, title it “Deployment runbook”,
add “Customer A connection issue” as its description, and annotate one message
with “Check the firewall first”. All these notes are searchable.

## Find, edit and remove

Open **Favorites** in the sidebar. Search message text, titles, descriptions,
individual notes, sender/conversation names or attachment filenames. Open an item
to see its ordered messages, edit its notes, or remove that favorite. Removing a
favorite leaves the original messages untouched. **View original** opens its exact
source when that source is still available.

Clearing ordinary chat history does not clear Favorites. An explicit per-message
expiry still applies to saved content; your notes and other saved messages remain.
A saved file card may have unavailable contents. Already available authorized files
up to 64 MiB can be retained independently; saving a card does not approve a
transfer or guarantee that a larger file is retained.

## Use Favorites on linked devices

Favorites and personal notes synchronize between compatible certified devices
when **Message history** is enabled on both. Retained attachment contents also
require **Attachments** on both. Removals synchronize as well. Local saves work
while offline; catch-up waits for a reachable compatible source. Older versions
may continue ordinary message sync without supporting Favorites.

Encrypted recovery backups currently exclude Favorites and their retained files.

See [Link devices](link-devices.md) and [History](history.md).
