# Troubleshoot a connection

Use the connection diagnostics to gather evidence before changing network settings.

## Open diagnostics

Choose **More → Connection**, or **Connection Diagnostics…** in the desktop tray menu. The diagnostics dialog separates overview, peers, logs and troubleshooting.

1. Check whether the app has usable network interfaces and a listening endpoint.
2. Review discovered peers and the freshness of their observations.
3. Use rescan when appropriate and inspect the result.
4. Open troubleshooting for platform-specific firewall guidance or the manual connection flow.
5. Inspect or export relevant log information only if you need it for further support.

A known endpoint may be a gateway or relay. Presence, authorization and successful transport are different facts. Avoid treating a listed IP as the person's physical location or proof of delivery.

## Share only what is needed

Logs and diagnostics can include network addresses and identifiers. Review them before sharing publicly. This help reader and its documentation MCP do not read or upload your diagnostics, chats or files.
